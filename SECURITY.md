# Security

Report a vulnerability privately through GitHub: open the repository's
**Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**. Don't open a public
issue for it.

Tracepanel is a development tool. It runs on your machine, keeps the traces in
your project's `.tracepanel/` and serves its console on `127.0.0.1`. Reports
about what it records, where it listens, or what another site or another
process on the machine can read or change through it are welcome.
